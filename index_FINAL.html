COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions are part of the process.

Here are a few common starting points. If your situation is different, that's exactly what the first conversation is for.

Can I talk with you before I'm ready to buy?

Absolutely. An early conversation can help you understand what to prepare, what questions to ask, and what your possible next steps may be.

Can you help me understand my estimated payment?

Yes. The calculator above is a starting point. A personalized scenario should also consider the specific loan, taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA dues, and other applicable costs.

Should I start with real estate or mortgage?

Either can be a good starting point. If you're unsure, start with a conversation and we can identify what information you need first.

Do I have to know exactly what I want before contacting you?

No. You can start with a question, an idea, or even “I don't know where to begin.”
